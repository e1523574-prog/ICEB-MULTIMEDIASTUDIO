BOOK / PUBLICATION

Classic Heritage

Ghana’s Wraparound Fashion Classic Heritage is a visual and educational fashion handbook that explores the origins, cultural significance, and modern influence of Ghanaian indigenous clothing. It traces traditional styles such as breast and waist covers, Queen Mother’s style, loin cloth, collar style, and raffia skirts, showing how these early forms of dress have influenced contemporary fashion styles such as fitted skirts, off-shoulder dresses, bikinis, halter dresses, pleated skirts, gathered skirts, and flare skirts. Through descriptive texts and photographic illustrations, the handbook connects Ghana’s traditional fashion heritage with present-day clothing, highlighting how indigenous creativity, culture, status, ceremony, and identity continue to inspire modern fashion. Overall, the handbook presents Ghanaian indigenous fashion as more than clothing, it is a representation of history, cultural identity, creativity, and heritage that continues to influence fashion today.

BOOK / PUBLICATION

Mastering Graphic Design AD

Mastering Graphic Design: Basics of Graphic Design is an educational textbook project designed to introduce students and aspiring designers to the fundamental principles and practices of graphic design. The book covers essential design elements such as line, shape, form, colour, texture, space, and value, as well as principles including balance, contrast, hierarchy, rhythm, and unity. The textbook also provides an in-depth exploration of colour theory and typography, including colour harmonies, colour psychology, colour modes for print and digital media, typeface classifications, typographic hierarchy, legibility, and readability. It further introduces Gestalt principles and visual perception, demonstrating how designers can organize visual information and create effective communication. As a design project, the textbook combines structured educational content with illustrations, diagrams, examples, and carefully organized layouts to make complex graphic design concepts easier to understand. Overall, it serves as a comprehensive guide for anyone interested in learning the fundamentals of graphic design.
